FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Is it allowed to rent out my society parking slot?+
In most societies, yes — as long as the slot is allotted to you and your RWA is informed. ParkNest handles RWA approvals and provides the society office with verified visitor details and gate passes. We recommend checking your society's parking policy once.
How much can I actually earn?+
Owners typically earn ₹2,000–₹8,000/month depending on city, location and hours available. Slots near IT parks, metro stations and commercial areas earn the most. Owners keep 85% of every booking.
What if the renter damages my car or blocks my slot?+
Every booking includes damage protection up to ₹50,000, and all drivers are KYC-verified with vehicle documents uploaded. If someone overstays, you can mark the slot blocked and our support team resolves it — you're never negotiating with a stranger.
How does the society security know who to let in?+
Each booking generates a time-bound QR gate pass shared with both the driver and the society office. The guard scans it at the boom barrier — valid only for the booked window and vehicle number.
When do I get paid as an owner?+
Within 24 hours of every completed booking, directly to your UPI or bank account. You can track all payouts in your dashboard and withdraw anytime.
Do you support two-wheelers and EV charging?+
Yes — two-wheeler, car, or both. Slots with EV charging points carry an EV badge and can charge a premium. Filters let parkers find exactly what they need.
